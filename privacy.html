Privacy

Privacy information for ClearExams, including browser storage, analytics and private tools.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Browser storage

Some ClearExams features save preferences, quiz history, progress or working data in your browser using local or session storage. Data saved this way remains on the device/browser unless a feature explicitly synchronises it to a configured cloud service.

Analytics

Public ClearExams pages may use Google Analytics to understand aggregate page usage and improve the site. Analytics providers may process standard device, browser and usage information under their own terms and privacy policies.

My Cases and private tools

My Cases is intended as a private workspace. Its cloud-sync features require authentication and use a separately configured backend. Private workspace pages are marked not to be indexed by search engines. Users should avoid entering information they are not authorised to store or process.

External links

ClearExams links to official recruiting bodies and other external resources. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
