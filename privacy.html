Privacy Notice

How ClearExams handles browser-based study data, analytics and advertising on the public website.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Information and browser storage

ClearExams quiz and study pages can store preferences, quiz attempts, progress, or draft work in your browser using local storage or session storage. These records normally remain on that browser until you clear site data, delete them in an available feature, or a specific tool explicitly synchronises to a configured service. Clearing site data may permanently erase local progress.

Google Analytics

Public pages may use Google Analytics to understand visitor traffic and site usage. Depending on the service configuration, Google can process device, browser, usage and approximate location information. Analytics is not a substitute for an exam application or login account. Learn about Google's data processing at Google Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense advertising and cookies

ClearExams uses Google AdSense advertising tags on selected pages. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons or similar identifiers to serve, measure and improve advertisements. Advertising may be personalised or non-personalised depending on settings, applicable requirements and user choices. These third parties may use information from visits to this and other websites for advertising. Learn how Google uses data on partner sites at Google's partner-sites information. You can review personalised advertising choices in Google My Ad Center and control or block cookies through your browser. In regions where an additional consent mechanism is required, the publisher must configure an appropriate consent-management solution before relying on consent for advertising; this notice alone is not a consent mechanism.

Private tools and other services

Separate tools, such as My Cases, may use authentication and a separately configured cloud backend. Their settings and access controls differ from public exam-preparation pages. Do not enter sensitive case information or personal documents unless authorised and the tool's specific data-handling arrangement has been checked.

External links and source documents

Official recruiting-body links and third-party educational sources are controlled by their respective publishers. Their privacy and cookie practices are separate from ClearExams.

Questions and corrections

For non-sensitive website errors, use Support and Corrections. This uses a public GitHub tracker. Never post personal details, private documents, credentials or confidential security information to a public issue. A dedicated private contact channel has not yet been published. See also Terms of Use and About ClearExams.
