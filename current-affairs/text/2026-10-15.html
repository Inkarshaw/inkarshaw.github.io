Economy

1. RBI reports a smaller current-account deficit in August 2026

The Reserve Bank of India's preliminary balance-of-payments data placed India's current-account deficit at USD 5.2 billion in August 2026, narrower than USD 6.1 billion a year earlier. A large merchandise-trade deficit was partly offset by net services receipts and transfers, while capital-account inflows produced an overall balance-of-payments surplus.

Exam-worthy facts

  • The merchandise-trade balance was a deficit of USD 26.6 billion, with exports of USD 44.7 billion and imports of USD 71.3 billion.
  • Net services receipts were USD 16.5 billion; services exports were USD 35.5 billion and imports USD 18.9 billion.
  • The capital account recorded net inflows of USD 50.6 billion and the overall balance was a surplus of USD 45.4 billion.
  • For April-August 2026, the current-account deficit was USD 18.3 billion and the overall balance was a surplus of USD 58.0 billion.

Exam connection: The balance of payments records a country's transactions with the rest of the world. The current account includes goods, services, primary income and transfers; reserve changes appear in the overall balance.

Source: RBI: Developments in India's Balance of Payments during August 2026, 15 October 2026

Economy and Banking

2. RBI clarifies Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve requirements

RBI issued frequently asked questions on the Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve framework. The reserve applies to specified rupee foreign-exchange derivatives used to buy foreign currency against INR for current-account hedges when the notional amount exceeds USD 2 million; it is a prudential liquidity requirement, not an interest-bearing asset.

Exam-worthy facts

  • The reserve equals 20% of the INR-equivalent notional amount of an eligible derivative.
  • Authorised Dealers must maintain it daily in their current account with RBI and report it through CIMS by 5 p.m. on T+1.
  • FERR earns no interest and cannot be treated as a High Quality Liquid Asset.
  • It does not apply when the derivative does not involve INR, when the user sells foreign currency, or when the underlying exposure is a capital-account transaction.
  • Contracts entered before the directions are exempt unless they are rolled over after the framework takes effect.

Exam connection: Authorised Dealer banks conduct permitted foreign-exchange business under FEMA. Prudential reserves can reduce liquidity risk but differ from CRR, SLR and Basel liquidity buffers.

Source: RBI FAQ: Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve requirements, updated 15 October 2026

Defence and Space

3. Defence Ministry signs GSAT-7C and Varunastra contracts worth about ₹4,106 crore

The Ministry of Defence signed two indigenous procurement contracts on 15 October 2026: GSAT-7C with NewSpace India Limited for secure Indian Air Force communications and Varunastra ship-launched anti-submarine torpedoes with Bharat Dynamics Limited. The combined value was approximately ₹4,106 crore.

Exam-worthy facts

  • The GSAT-7C contract is valued at ₹2,563 crore and was signed with NewSpace India Limited, Bengaluru.
  • The satellite is intended to provide secure, resilient and seamless communication support to the Indian Air Force.
  • The Varunastra contract is valued at ₹1,543 crore and was signed with Bharat Dynamics Limited, Hyderabad.
  • Varunastra is an indigenous ship-launched anti-submarine torpedo developed by the Naval Science and Technological Laboratory.

Exam connection: NewSpace India Limited is the commercial arm of the Department of Space. NSTL is a DRDO laboratory working on underwater weapons and related naval systems.

Source: PIB Ministry of Defence: Contracts for GSAT-7C and Varunastra, 15 October 2026

Infrastructure and Geography

4. Five Ladakh development projects worth about ₹3,350 crore inaugurated

The Prime Minister inaugurated or dedicated five projects in Ladakh through video conference. The package combines air connectivity, a strategic road, hydropower transmission, high-altitude water storage and rock-art conservation; the government described the completed package as worth about ₹3,350 crore.

Exam-worthy facts

  • The new terminal at Kushok Bakula Rinpoche Airport in Leh cost about ₹650 crore and raises annual passenger capacity from 10 lakh to 35 lakh.
  • The terminal uses geothermal heating along with solar energy for Ladakh's cold climate.
  • The approximately ₹2,500-crore Nimmu-Padum-Darcha road cuts the stated journey time from 12 hours to about three hours.
  • The package includes the Hanu hydroelectric project with its transmission network and 40 reservoirs under Project Amrit Him Sarovar.
  • India's first Petroglyph Conservation Park was inaugurated in Leh to preserve historic rock art.

Exam connection: Ladakh became a Union Territory in 2019. The Nimmu-Padum-Darcha axis improves connectivity through Zanskar and has both civilian and strategic importance.

Source: PIB PMO: Five development projects inaugurated in Ladakh, 15 October 2026

Science and Technology

5. NITI Aayog releases a five-pillar data-centre evaluation framework

NITI Aayog's Frontier Tech Hub released a decision-support framework for states evaluating data-centre proposals. It is guidance rather than a new regulator: states are asked to test infrastructure readiness, resource sustainability, economic value and implementation capacity before offering project support.

Exam-worthy facts

  • The five pillars are Power and Energy; Water and Cooling; Siting and Environmental Sustainability; Economic and Strategic Value; and Policy, Governance and Implementation.
  • India's data-centre capacity was estimated to have risen from about 375 MW in 2020 to approximately 1.5 GW.
  • Recommendations include grid-readiness checks, renewable energy and storage, basin-level water-stress assessment, rainwater harvesting and waste lifecycle plans.
  • The framework recommends linking state incentives to measurable outcomes and distinguishing colocation facilities from active-compute services.

Exam connection: NITI Aayog is the Union government's policy think tank. Data centres are physical facilities hosting compute, storage and networking systems and can be intensive users of electricity and cooling water.

Source: PIB NITI Aayog: Data Centre Evaluation Framework, 15 October 2026

Digital Governance

6. BHASHINI and Manipur sign MoU for multilingual digital services

The Digital India BHASHINI Division and the Government of Manipur signed an MoU at the BHASHINI Rajyam workshop held in Imphal on 14 October and reported on 15 October. The agreement provides a cooperation framework; priority public-service use cases, language-data needs and platform integrations are still to be identified and tested.

Exam-worthy facts

  • The MoU focuses on language technology, stronger language resources and multilingual government and citizen services.
  • The next phase is to identify priority use cases, assess data requirements and validate solutions with language experts and local communities.
  • BHASHINI is described as Language-First Digital Public Infrastructure under the Digital India Corporation.
  • The platform supports 36 Indian languages for text and 23 Indian languages for voice, according to the release.

Exam connection: Digital Public Infrastructure comprises interoperable digital systems used at population scale. BHASHINI applies AI models, datasets and APIs to reduce language barriers in digital services.

Source: PIB MeitY: BHASHINI Division-Manipur MoU, 15 October 2026

Tribal Affairs and Culture

7. First Working Group meeting charts roadmap for tribal-language preservation

The Ministry of Tribal Affairs convened the first meeting of its Working Group on Tribal Languages. The group will guide documentation, research, digital preservation, multilingual education and community participation, while Adi Vaani is being developed as a national tribal-language repository.

Exam-worthy facts

  • Adi Vaani, developed by IIT Delhi with the Ministry, provides text translation in nine tribal languages plus speech, OCR and video-subtitling features.
  • It provides searchable access to more than 300 primers prepared by Tribal Research Institutes.
  • State TRIs have contributed about 13.9 lakh text sentences in ten languages and roughly 300 hours of speech data.
  • The proposed roadmap includes a national baseline, community-led oral-history documentation, digital archives and mother-tongue multilingual learning materials.

Exam connection: Article 29 protects the right of citizens to conserve a distinct language, script or culture. The Eighth Schedule separately lists constitutionally recognised languages and does not cover every tribal language.

Source: PIB Ministry of Tribal Affairs: First Working Group meeting on tribal languages, 15 October 2026

Tamil Nadu Polity

8. Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar assumes office as Governor of Tamil Nadu

Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar assumed office as Governor of Tamil Nadu at Lok Bhavan, Chennai, on 15 October 2026. The President's 12 October appointment order had transferred him from Keralam and stated that the appointment would take effect from the date he assumed charge.

Exam-worthy facts

  • The office of Governor is provided for in Article 153 of the Constitution.
  • Under Article 155, the Governor is appointed by the President by warrant under the President's hand and seal.
  • Article 159 prescribes the oath or affirmation made by a Governor before entering office.
  • Arlekar had earlier been directed to discharge the functions of Governor of Tamil Nadu before this regular appointment.

Exam connection: A Governor is the constitutional head of a state. Articles 153-162 cover the office, appointment, term, qualifications, oath and executive power of the Governor.

Source: Lok Bhavan Tamil Nadu: Governor assumed office in Chennai, 15 October 2026

Rural Development

9. National SARAS Mela highlights women-led rural enterprises in Vijayawada

President Droupadi Murmu attended the National SARAS Mela in Vijayawada, organised by the Union Ministry of Rural Development with the Andhra Pradesh government. The event connects women Self-Help Group entrepreneurs directly with consumers and is presented as a market-access platform rather than a new welfare entitlement.

Exam-worthy facts

  • Around 1,500 women SHG entrepreneurs and Lakhpati Didis from 34 States and Union Territories are participating.
  • More than 650 stalls are showcasing products, and the mela remains open through 31 October 2026.
  • SARAS platforms support market access for rural enterprises associated with Self-Help Groups.
  • The President stressed control over income and assets, community support for care work and climate-resilient rural enterprises.

Exam connection: DAY-NRLM promotes social mobilisation, financial inclusion and livelihoods through women Self-Help Groups. SARAS fairs provide offline marketing opportunities for products made by SHG members.

Source: PIB President's Secretariat: National SARAS Mela in Vijayawada, 15 October 2026

Environment

10. G.B. Pant Society meeting releases four Himalayan research publications

The Union Environment Minister chaired the 27th meeting of the G.B. Pant Society of Himalayan Environment and Development and released four publications on Himalayan biodiversity, glaciers, digital biodiversity registers and wetlands. The meeting emphasised access-and-benefit sharing and community participation, but did not announce a new statutory scheme.

Exam-worthy facts

  • The publications cover the Bhagirathi Eco-sensitive Zone, the Gangotri glaciated system, e-PBR governance and Ramsar wetlands of the Indian Himalayan Region.
  • People's Biodiversity Registers document local biological resources and associated knowledge at the community level.
  • Access and benefit-sharing seeks fair sharing of gains arising from the use of biological resources and associated knowledge.

Exam connection: India's biodiversity governance is anchored in the Biological Diversity Act. Biodiversity Management Committees at local-body level prepare People's Biodiversity Registers, while Ramsar sites are wetlands of international importance.

Source: PIB Environment Ministry: 27th G.B. Pant Society meeting, 15 October 2026
