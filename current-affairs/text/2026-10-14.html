Defence & International Cooperation

1. Indian Navy's first command of CMF Task Force 154 concludes

The Indian Navy was scheduled to hand over command of Combined Task Force 154 (CTF 154) at Manama, Bahrain, on 14 October 2026. India took command on 11 February 2026, its first leadership of a task force under the Combined Maritime Forces (CMF).

  • CTF 154 focuses on multinational maritime training and capacity building.
  • The CMF partnership has 47 nations, according to the Defence Ministry's release.
  • India became an associate partner in 2022 and a full member in 2023.
  • India's command period involved personnel from 39 countries and over 7,800 training hours.

Remember: CTF 154 = maritime training; handover venue = Manama, Bahrain.

Primary source: Ministry of Defence, PIB, 14 Oct 2026.

Science, Technology & Innovation

2. ₹151 crore RDI funding for YETI heavy-lift autonomous aircraft

The Technology Development Board (TDB), under the Department of Science and Technology, signed an agreement with ideaForge Technology Limited for ₹151 crore in debt financing through the Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) Fund. The proposed project's total cost is ₹302 crore.

  • YETI is a proposed autonomous hybrid aircraft combining vertical take-off and landing with fixed-wing flight.
  • Design targets: payload up to 200 kg, range up to 200 km and altitude up to 6,500 m.
  • The project aims to progress from Technology Readiness Level 4 to Level 9.
  • These are design targets; the release does not establish that the finished aircraft already meets them.

Remember: TDB is linked to the Department of Science and Technology; VTOL means vertical take-off and landing.

Primary source: Ministry of Science & Technology, PIB, 14 Oct 2026.

Economy, MSME & Agriculture-related Industry

3. Coir industry: exports and employment highlighted

A Ministry of MSME background release highlighted India's coir industry. Coir is a natural fibre obtained from the outer husk of a coconut and is used for ropes, mats, mattresses and other products.

  • Reported coir exports were ₹6,614.40 crore in FY 2025–26, compared with ₹1,630.33 crore in FY 2014–15.
  • The ministry described this as approximately 305.7% growth over the period.
  • More than 21,000 Udyam-registered units were reported, employing around 2.3 lakh people in that registered group.
  • Coir Vikas Yojana is a central sector scheme run by the Ministry of MSME for the sector's development.

Remember: Coir = coconut husk fibre; Coir Vikas Yojana = MSME sector programme.

Primary source: Ministry of MSME, PIB, 14 Oct 2026.

Economy & International Organisations

4. Finance Minister scheduled for IMF–World Bank meetings in Bangkok

The Union Finance Ministry announced that Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman would visit Bangkok, Thailand, 14–18 October 2026, to take part in the annual meetings of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and World Bank Group.

  • The announced schedule also included meetings of G20 and G7 finance ministers and central bank governors.
  • The bilateral meeting programme included counterparts from Japan, Switzerland, Luxembourg and Bhutan, among others.
  • As of this afternoon edition, these are scheduled engagements, not reported outcomes.

Remember: IMF and World Bank are separate international financial institutions; both were created under the Bretton Woods framework.

Primary source: Ministry of Finance, PIB, 14 Oct 2026.
