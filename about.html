About ClearExams and how its exam-preparation resources are organised.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

What ClearExams is

ClearExams is an independent educational website that organises competitive-exam preparation into practical paths: official syllabus references, study material, daily quizzes, previous-year questions, current-affairs revision and notification tracking.

How information is presented

Exam dates, eligibility, vacancies and recruitment rules can change. Where possible, ClearExams links to the recruiting body's official website or official notification so learners can verify current requirements before applying.

Focus

The site currently includes resources for TNPSC, TNUSRB, UPSC, SSC, banking and railway examinations, with additional tools and topics added over time.
