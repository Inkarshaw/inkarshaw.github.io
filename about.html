About

About ClearExams and how its exam-preparation resources are organised.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

What ClearExams is

ClearExams is an independent educational website that organises competitive-exam preparation into practical paths: official syllabus references, study material, daily quizzes, previous-year questions, current-affairs revision and notification tracking.

How information is presented

Exam dates, eligibility, vacancies and recruitment rules can change. Where possible, ClearExams links to the recruiting body's official website or official notification so learners can verify current requirements before applying.

Focus

The site currently includes resources for TNPSC, TNUSRB, UPSC, SSC, banking and railway examinations, with additional tools and topics added over time.

Who is responsible for the website?

ClearExams is an independent educational publisher. Content currently carries an organisation-level byline where an individual author has not been verified. We do not claim unverified founder degrees, government roles, ranks or staff qualifications. Real contributor biographies can be added with consent and verifiable credentials.

Sources and corrections

Exam dates and rules must be checked with the official recruiting body. Question banks distinguish original learning exercises from sourced past-paper material. Some older textbook source links are still under editorial review. Read our editorial standards, sourcing policy and correction instructions, and use the calendar to check board-specific review dates.

Can students trust reviews on this site?

We do not display invented testimonials, fabricated learner counts, imaginary selection statistics or endorsements. Genuine experience reports should only appear with permission and appropriate disclosure. Until then, students can inspect the material and try a free 12-question sample without signing up.

How to report a mistake

You can open a public content correction issue with the page, specific error and an official or textbook source. Do not put private documents or personal contact details in public issues. For data-handling details, see Privacy.
