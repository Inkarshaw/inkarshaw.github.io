Editorial standards and source transparency

Students should be able to see which information comes from a recruiting body, which questions are original or adapted, and when an exam date was actually checked.

Last updated: 28 October 2026 · Published by: ClearExams (organisation-level editorial byline).

Who publishes the content?

ClearExams is an independent exam-preparation website. Its public material is published under the ClearExams organisational byline unless a particular page identifies a real, verified contributor. This byline does not claim any person has a specific degree, rank, government position or appointment.

Named contributor biographies and credentials should be published only when the contributor has agreed and the information can be checked. The absence of a named author should not be mistaken for a verified professional qualification. ClearExams is not affiliated with TNPSC, UPSC, SSC, RRB, IBPS or TNUSRB.

Source priority

For recruitment dates, eligibility, fees, seats, admit cards, syllabus and exam scheme, the original recruiting body's current notification and official amendments take priority. The exam calendar is a convenient index, not the official application service. Source URLs and last-reviewed dates should be displayed where available. A scheduled automated check is not the same as evidence that an official source was successfully examined.

For school-textbook-based questions, the applicable state textbook and edition take priority. Textbook page references require edition-specific verification. Some older references are still under review; an unverified link should not be presented as proof that the source was fully checked.

For current affairs, prefer original public documents and statements, and distinguish announcement dates, effective dates, proposals, implemented changes and forecasts. Corrections should preserve that distinction.

How practice questions are labelled

The NCERT School Book Quiz contains original, English-language concept practice, not a reproduction of NCERT exercises or textbook pages. It does not claim official NCERT endorsement or exact chapter/page alignment. Use the official NCERT catalogue for copyrighted textbooks and the latest editions.

Original practice questions are educational exercises, not official past-paper extracts. An item labelled “Previous Year Question” should have an exam, year and suitable verifiable source information. Paraphrased versions should be marked as adapted when necessary and should not be represented as a verbatim original. Every four-option question should have one defensible answer, plausible alternatives and an explanation that addresses why the answer is correct.

The instant sample quiz contains 12 original demonstration questions. Its results are a practice aid only; it makes no claim to predict selection, percentile, ranking or examination outcomes.

Reviewing bilingual content

English and Tamil questions should express the same task, maintain accurate technical terms and preserve the same correct option. Distractors must be relevant and grammatically sensible. If an official exam uses an ambiguous question or later corrects its key, editorial material should be updated and dated where the change affects guidance.

How freshness is shown

Board-specific review records are published on the exam calendar. An overdue review stays visible until an official source is actually examined. A source verified today does not prove that every notice on a board's website has been checked today. Whenever possible, individual entries state a check date. Historical or closed notices may remain for context and should not be displayed as open applications.

Corrections and feedback

To report an error, include the page URL, question or notice ID, the contested statement, the proposed correction and a primary source if available. ClearExams' public GitHub issue form offers a public route for a reproducible report. Do not submit personal identifiers, admit card information, passwords, phone numbers or private documents in a public issue.

The site's existing quality review backlog includes textbook source-link checks. Not every question or notification is continuously validated, and a quality-check workflow passing does not independently prove that all factual assertions are correct.

Testimonials and results

ClearExams does not publish invented testimonials or imply a learner earned a rank because they used this site. A future testimonial should use the real participant's informed permission, identify material conflicts of interest and avoid unverifiable selection or success claims. No fabricated review count, success rate or institutional endorsement is presented here.

Privacy and analytics

Some practice records and settings may be stored in the browser; other site features and analytics have separate configurations. Review ClearExams' privacy page for the existing disclosure. Educational resources should never require learners to share sensitive documents merely to access an introductory sample.

Important: The authoritative answer to an eligibility, deadline, application status or exam venue question is the recruiting body's current notice or admit-card instructions. ClearExams is an independent study aid and cannot guarantee results.
