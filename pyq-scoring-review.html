Correction and publication process

First check the original PDF question number, all options and figures. Next compare the correct paper version and latest official key. Resolve ambiguous wording or official ALL status; only then update the question record, regenerate this audit and rerun automated quiz/mobile tests. A mechanical validation pass cannot replace an editorial source review.
