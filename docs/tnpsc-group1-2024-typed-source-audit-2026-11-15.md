- Items `Q40/Q43/Q47` and other mathematical expressions have additional exact-typesetting checks pending; long texts, source historical wording and passage Q194 remain editorially adapted.
- No mapped answer keys exist in this typed JSON. The final key PDF is linked as a **reference**, never used to guess marks. `accepted: []`, `keyStatus: unmapped` and `grading: not-graded` remain enforced.
