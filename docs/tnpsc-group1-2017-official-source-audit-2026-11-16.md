## Source and editorial cautions

- **Q1** refers to satellite launches in 2016, not a present-day ISRO launch statistic.
- **Q7** uses categorical statements about minerals and ores, which should be read in their extraction-economics context.
- **Q11** presents an unusual assertion–reason option formulation. The handwritten tick is not sufficient for key certification.
- **Q13** associates the lepromin test with leprosy. Historically it is principally used for classification/immunological response, not a definitive diagnostic test; do not turn a historical question into medical advice.
- **Q17** prints Ravi Shankar alongside Sangeet Natak Academy without specifying the type of association; source wording retained as a matching exercise for review.
- **Q124–Q125** source exact sequence and printed A–D options are established from page 49; handwritten ticks remain ungraded.

**Items needing special review rather than incorrect auto-scoring:**

- **Q30:** 'Increase in working class' is the source's printed phrase for demographic dividend. The adapted stem clarifies the intended working-age population, without claiming verbatim reproduction.
- **Q31:** The source gives 'largest share of energy consumed' but does not specify whether it means electricity, total energy or the statistical year. It is not safe to use as a current objective fact without external statistics.
- **Q39:** The English source calls a Concurrent List subject 'Criminal procedure code'; the adapted option uses the constitutional Schedule's term 'Criminal procedure'.
- **Q41 (high priority):** The statement about appointment of Executive Magistrates in consultation with the High Court (A) is potentially false; the claimed absence of High Court revisional jurisdiction (C) is also potentially false. A single-choice correct-answer key based on this scan's handwritten tick would be misleading. **Do not grade**. Compare legal provisions and authoritative final key before certifying.
- **Q45:** First Lokayukta law versus first operational institution requires historical distinction.
- **Q46:** The committee-matching labels 'revitalisation' and 'strengthening' Panchayati Raj overlap. This is a historical matching item; leave ungraded until committee-purpose phrasing is independently verified.
