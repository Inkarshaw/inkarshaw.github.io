## Source and editorial cautions

- **Q1** refers to satellite launches in 2016, not a present-day ISRO launch statistic.
- **Q7** uses categorical statements about minerals and ores, which should be read in their extraction-economics context.
- **Q11** presents an unusual assertion–reason option formulation. The handwritten tick is not sufficient for key certification.
- **Q13** associates the lepromin test with leprosy. Historically it is principally used for classification/immunological response, not a definitive diagnostic test; do not turn a historical question into medical advice.
- **Q17** prints Ravi Shankar alongside Sangeet Natak Academy without specifying the type of association; source wording retained as a matching exercise for review.
- **Q124–Q125** source exact sequence and printed A–D options are established from page 49; handwritten ticks remain ungraded.
