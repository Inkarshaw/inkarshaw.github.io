## Visual and source-fidelity exceptions

- **Q41, Q96, Q104:** matching-list tables have been converted to self-contained readable prose with the four original-order code strings; they still require a full visual comparison with printed columns and exact numeric codes.
- **Q58–60 and Q64–66:** source text is duplicated across interactive questions, although the paper prints each passage once. The independently typed passage and original scan still need word-for-word comparison.
- **Q105:** printed-study alternatives appear semantically inconsistent with the chemical-light stem. The four recorded source choices remain unchanged, with a recorded issue. No alternative or answer was fabricated to fix it.
- Source pages are recorded only where directly checked, not inferred. There is **no known mandatory map/diagram** in the recovered CDS II 2010 question positions; this does not establish the absence of illustrations in an official source edition.
- English OCR/typed reprints can alter punctuation, line breaks, special symbols, and capitalization. A complete official-booklet visual review remains necessary.
