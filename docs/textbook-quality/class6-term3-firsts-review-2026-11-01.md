## Candidate facts excluded or deferred

- **Sri Gupta** as early Gupta ruler/founder: already covered by existing Firsts Quiz F077; do **not** add a duplicate.
- **Aryabhatta** as the “first” Indian astronomer to assert the Earth's axial rotation: original book uses that wording, but the historic absolute-first qualification has not been independently researched. Do not import an unqualified worldwide/Indian first; the same unit contains some potentially questionable historical source attribution.
- **Rudradaman’s Girnar inscription** as the “first inscription in chaste Sanskrit”: wording needs specialist qualification against earlier Sanskrit inscriptions; not imported.
- **Muziris** as Pliny’s “first emporium of India”: contextual literary attribution, not verified as an objective absolute “first” commercial settlement; not imported.
- **Early Chinese navigation**: the quiz reports the specific textbook's lodestone/mariner account, not a contested universal history of the compass.
- The textbook contains other uses of “first” in activity instructions; keyword matches are not automatically new questions.
