## Excluded/deferred candidates and limits

- London's alleged **first million-person city**: not added; earlier cities and modern population definitions make an unqualified first misleading.
- **First use of chemical symbols**: not framed as Dalton being the first person ever to mark substances; F158 narrowly refers to specific atom-quantity symbols.
- **Razia as first woman ruler**: F161 is strictly limited to the **Delhi Sultanate**; avoid an absolute worldwide or all-India claim.
- **The first Islamic empire in India**, assorted dynasty and conquest claims, and QR / learning activity language are not mechanically imported as Firsts without historical qualification.
- Women's Empowerment original Class 7 unit is in another term and is **not** certified by reviewing Term I.
- Original-source quiz coverage for Class 7 Terms II/III, and all non-Firsts content in Term I, require separate audit.
