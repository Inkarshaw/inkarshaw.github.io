## Source tracking and quality rules

- Each published question must contain four distinct English/Tamil options, one valid answer, explanation in both languages and at least one link to a source book.
- The designation must be scoped accurately (e.g. the Class 11 "Father of Printing Press" reference is in the context of early Tamil printing; "Father of the Nation" is a historical form of address, not a formally awarded constitutional title).
- Deduplicate across books: one person/title fact is asked once, while `source_references` retains all book-level origins.
- Do not add questions based solely on a webpage's suggested trivia list; reference an actual TN textbook chapter or unit bank and review the title's scope.
- Preserve per-book original source path and relevant printed page, where known. Book editions or OCR/translations still pending should not be presented as fully confirmed.
