| **F182** | Year India's national **family-planning programme** began: **1952** | Science **262** | Science **259** (selected original Tamil embedded text, page-image signoff pending) | [National Health Mission official history](https://www.nhm.gov.in/index1.php?lang=1&level=1&lid=398&sublinkid=365) |

**Important distinctions:** Apsara was a **research reactor**, not a commercial electricity-generating power plant. Tarapur is the first nuclear **power station**, not the country's first reactor. The 1948 AEC and the Bhabha Atomic Research Centre are distinct institutions, despite a misleading passage in the printed book equating the institutions; DAE records the AEC's 1958 reconstitution separately. The textbook's older count and distribution of reactors is time-bound, not current 2026 information. The National Health Mission documents 1952; F182 asks for the launch year, not an unqualified global policy superlative.
