## Textbook provenance and editorial caveats

The original NCERT chapter PDF is **https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/jesc105.pdf**. A searchable *2025–26* reprint of the chapter at https://files.supercop.in/ncert-books/2025/class10/jesc105.pdf was consulted for chapter concepts and section page ranges. The direct original NCERT PDF request timed out and PDF screenshots could not be obtained during this run; therefore **printed-page correspondence has not been visually certified** and the 2026–27 reprint/edition has not been independently reviewed.

Questions are **new, ClearExams-authored** comprehension and application MCQs, not transcriptions of NCERT end-of-chapter exercises. The Tamil explanations and choices are ClearExams translations; they require native-language editorial proofreading before any final certification. Every new set is explicitly labelled `editorial-review-pending`. Published starter-level practice is **not** a claim of every possible fact/question in this chapter having been extracted.
