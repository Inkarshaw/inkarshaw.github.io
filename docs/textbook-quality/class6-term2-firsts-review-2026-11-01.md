## What was excluded, and why

- **Mauryan Empire “first empire” / Nandas “first empire builders”:** broad historically contested superlatives, and the edition itself presents both claims. No unqualified “India's first empire” question was added.
- **First Jain Tirthankara as an objectively documented historical person:** question F068 explicitly scopes the belief to **Jain tradition**; no historical dating is invented.
- **Any “first computer/electricity/cell” candidate without source passage:** no additions from generic keyword searches alone.
- **Claim of exhaustive Class 6 Term II question coverage:** keyword matching can miss facts expressed without the terms “first”, “முதல்”, or English synonyms; images/tables and exercises require manual inspection.

## Check details and remaining book certification blockers

- Four original SCERT PDFs were fetched from their **full URLs** and fingerprinted, and **every one of the 420 PDF pages produced some machine-extractable text**.
- The 2024-labelled independent English and Tamil transcriptions for both subjects were also checked as secondary references. The Tamil PDFs contain font encoding artifacts in portions of their text layers; visually inspecting certain source pages was necessary and confirms not all machine text is reliable.
- The term review targets selected passages (Science Unit 5 Cell, History Units 2–3, Civics Unit 1 National Symbols); it does **not** include exhaustive human page-by-page review of all Science and Social Science chapters, figures, tables, maps and end exercises.
