**Grading safeguards (do not claim the official key is itself changed):**

- **Q21:** The original matching list labels Bardoli Satyagraha as **1929** (option C), conflicting with the historical **1928** movement. The official 2015 final key marks C, but the source item is excluded from adapted-text scoring. Government of India Bardoli history: <https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/printrelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=3&relid=153068>.
- **Q22:** The source matches **Shuja-ud-Daula** with *Nawab of Bengal*, although he ruled **Awadh/Oudh**. Official choice C is preserved for transparency but no study grade is assigned. Uttar Pradesh Government district history: <https://lucknow.nic.in/nawabs-of-lucknow/>.
- **Q26:** TNPSC official key marks *Mahatma Gandhi* (C) as the one who bestowed the 'Sardar' title; multiple historical accounts say the local Bardoli peasants, particularly women, conferred it after the 1928 satyagraha. The printed options omit that attribution, so no study grade is assigned. Government of India reference: <https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/printrelease.aspx?lang=2&reg=3&relid=153068>; historical biography: <https://indembkwt.gov.in/pdf/english_exhibition_sardar_patel_the_architect_of_unification1_compressed-nn.pdf>.
